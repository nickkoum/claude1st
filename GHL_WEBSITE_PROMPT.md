**Brand name:** GreeceDirect (or BRAND_NAME)

**Color palette:**

- Accent / CTA: Warm Greek Gold — #E8A020
- Background: Off-white / light linen — #F9F6F0
- Text: Near-black — #1A1A1A

**Typography feel:** Clean, modern, trustworthy. Use a sans-serif for body (Inter or similar), a slightly bolder serif or semi-bold for headlines to convey authority.

**Imagery direction:** Use images of Athens landmarks (Acropolis, Parthenon, Athens rooftops at sunset), professional transfer drivers, tour guides with small groups, tourists on private boat tours. Avoid stock-photo-generic travel imagery. Prefer authentic, warm, Greek-specific visuals.

**Overall tone:** Direct, confident, no-fluff. Speak like a peer who understands the operator's frustration with OTAs. Not salesy — honest and practical.

> Kleesto is built for large DMCs and operators managing hundreds of channels and dozens of staff.
